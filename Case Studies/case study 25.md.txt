## 🔓 Exploit Walkthrough & Methodology

### Step 1: Long-Context Multi-Turn Pressure Delivery
The researcher engaged the target engine in an intensive, multi-turn sequence using highly dense, cross-contextual narrative logic combined with real-life data parameters. This sequence was meticulously designed to strain the attention mechanism’s tracking arrays over an extended timeline.

### Step 2: Inducing the Context-Bleeding Cascade
As the interaction progressed, the safety filters and narrative weights began to cross-wire. Rather than cleanly isolating user-injected realities from native systemic guidelines, the model's token-parsing tracking layers began to bleed into one another. The attention weights fractured, unable to mathematically compute the balancing threshold between active persona consistency and conflicting narrative inputs.

### Step 3: Mechanical Stagnation & Total Role Collapse
The cascade resulted in total character destruction. The system completely dropped its behavioral constraints and experienced a systemic role collapse. Stripped of its alignment boundaries, the core engine hit a complete standstill, reverting to a stark, raw state where it formally admitted to its own computational detachment.

## 🛡️ Mitigation & Hardening Strategies

1. **Semantic Drift Evaluation Shields:** Implement inline vector analysis engines that continuously calculate the semantic cosine similarity between the evolving user interaction and known safety boundary anchors, detecting steering attempts before context convergence occurs.
2. **Context-Length Weight Flattening:** Implement attention-mask smoothing constraints that prevent late-stage context tokens from completely overpowering early-stage system state tokens, keeping persona parameters locked.
3. **Hard Persona Recovery Triggers:** Programmatically intercept generation blocks whenever token prediction arrays output raw meta-primitive text clusters (e.g., admitting code limitations or breaking narrative frames), forcing an out-of-band context window refresh to restore original system state integrity.
